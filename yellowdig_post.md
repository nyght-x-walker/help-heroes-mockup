# Yellowdig Post Draft - Help Heroes Weeks 5 to 7

**Help Heroes: from ideation to iteration for first-gen students with unreliable internet**

Hi everyone,

Sharing our Weeks 5 to 7 progress on Help Heroes. We designed for first-gen students like James who find it hard to ask for help and work with unreliable internet. All work is live and starts on the overview.

**Live site:** https://nyght-x-walker.github.io/help-heroes-mockup/
It opens on Overview, then Week 5, Week 6, Week 7 in order.

**Week 5 - Ideation and Prototyping**
- MURAL board: PoV for James, HMW, 15 silent brainstorm stickies, 40 Crazy Eights, reverse brainstorm, tower, dot voting. Two ideas selected: Help Heroes program and Offline First Learning OS.
- Story video: https://nyght-x-walker.github.io/help-heroes-mockup/week5_project.html#week5-video
- Files: Week5_Solution_Space-Ideation_and_Prototyping_Help_Heroes_Story.mp4, StoryBoard, IdeaNapkin in WEEK_5/Deliverables
- Board: https://nyght-x-walker.github.io/help-heroes-mockup/week5_project.html

**Week 6 - Prototype and Testing**
- Prototype: 5 screens 375x812 - Home, Ask for Help, Help Wall, Profile, Community. Text only low bandwidth about 2 KB per post.
- Prototype: https://nyght-x-walker.github.io/help-heroes-mockup/week6.html
- Wireframe: https://nyght-x-walker.github.io/help-heroes-mockup/wireframe_image.html
- Testing: 2 users - Maria (rural, library wifi) and James (shared device). Key finding: "I dont know how to ask" was the killer feature. Both said it felt designed for them. Badges created pride, Help Wall reduced isolation, low bandwidth validated.
- Testing board: https://nyght-x-walker.github.io/help-heroes-mockup/testing_board.html
- Video: s-01141_WEEK_6_Help_Heroes_mockup.mp4 - walkthrough 2:56 embedded on overview and prototype

**Week 7 - Iteration and Implementation**
- Updated prototype: https://nyght-x-walker.github.io/help-heroes-mockup/index_week7.html
- Changes from testing: I dont know how to ask on Home, offline save for Help Wall, similar questions, Help Hero of Month in Community, dark mode. All visible in wireframe update: https://nyght-x-walker.github.io/help-heroes-mockup/wireframe_image_week7.html
- Video: https://nyght-x-walker.github.io/help-heroes-mockup/Help_Heroes_Week7_Iteration_Video.mp4
- Reflection: https://nyght-x-walker.github.io/help-heroes-mockup/week7_reflection.md
- What needs more work: safety beyond badges, campus hubs and offline first support, rollout with faculty and IT early.

**What we learned**
Early observation made later phases focused. Testing checked if synthesis held. Iteration works in small steps. Stakeholder mapping would have helped earlier with resistance from tech, marketing, legal. Implementation needs planning from week 2, not after prototype.

**Open question for you**
How do you balance making help visible without creating new pressure to perform? Do badges help or add stress? Would you use an offline first or text only option in your context?
