Design Challenge

Redesign the online learning experience for first-generation university students in a world where high-speed internet is unreliable.

Focus on James, a first-generation student who often apologizes for needing help and finds the unwritten rules of university hard to follow. The board below shows how we explored ideas for him.

01 Section 1

Point of View and HMW Question

Who we design for and the question that guided ideation.

PoV Statement

1I met James, a first-generation student who has learned to apologize for late work, for missed meetings, for needing help, and who has internalized the belief that he is a burden to the system.

2I was inspired to discover that he desperately wants to ask for help but does not even know what to ask, because the institutional knowledge that some of his peers inherited from their families is a foreign language to him.

3It is all about helping James navigate and belong in the university environment without feeling like an outsider who has to apologize for existing.

First gen Unreliable internet Hidden curriculum Belonging
? How Might We

How might we help first-generation students like James navigate online learning, ask for help confidently, and feel they belong without constantly apologizing for their circumstances?

Navigate Ask confidently Belong

Why this HMW matters

It keeps the focus on the student experience and on making help seeking feel normal and supported.

04 Section 4

Reverse Brainstorming

First we asked how to make things worse, then we reversed those ideas.

💀

How to Make It Worse

Worst case ideas on purpose

💀

Make help seeking even more bureaucratic

Add 5 forms, require faculty signatures, 2 week waiting list before you can ask a question.

💀

Require high speed internet for everything

Stream every lecture in 4K, no downloads, auto logout if bandwidth drops.

💀

Remove all support systems

No mentors, no office hours, no FAQs, figure it out yourself.

💡

Reversed Ideas

Flipped into better solutions

🦸

Help Heroes Program

CELEBRATE

Celebrate and reward help seeking. Turn bureaucracy into badges and pride.

📱

Offline First Everything

All materials work without internet. Downloads, text only, auto sync when reconnected.

✨

Invisible Support

Help comes to students proactively. Gentle nudges, no begging needed.

These two reversed ideas became the prototypes for Week 6.
