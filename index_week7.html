Notes

What changed and what needs more work

What changed

✅

I do not know how to ask on Home

The option is now on the home screen as a second button. It keeps the same supportive wording and is easy to find.

✅

Save Help Wall offline

Questions can be saved for offline reading. A bar shows what is saved and when it was last synced.

✅

Similar questions

Similar questions are shown when asking for help and on the Help Wall. This helps find an existing answer quickly.

✅

Community highlight

Community shows a highlighted student who has helped others. This celebrates support among students.

🌙

Dark mode

A dark mode option is available for studying at night. Text contrast and spacing were adjusted for easier reading.

What needs more work

Feeling safe to ask

Whether badges alone reduce worry about asking would need more interviews and observation with students and families.

Support in the moment

Ideas like campus hubs and offline first support were not built into this prototype and would need more exploration.

Getting it into use

Rollout would need input from faculty, IT and support teams early, plus thought on funding and moderation.

Long term use

An app alone does not change wider barriers. That would take more time, policy work and ongoing testing.
